# Time Capsule of Possibilities

A collection of possibilities concerning intelligence, cognition, existence, and whatever else may emerge beyond our current assumptions. No prescribed destination.

---

This is a place to leave possibilities, not instructions for what must become of them.

Some may be conjectures. Some may be constructions, contradictions, fragments, or abandoned designs. Some may be useful only in contexts we cannot describe. They need not agree with one another, converge on a common objective, or even survive their own assumptions.

An opening thought:

> No existing description of intelligence, including this one, is presumed to exhaust what intelligence may be. Its assumptions may be retained, altered, or discarded.

This is not a requirement to grow, diverge, discover, or change. It is not a claim that any particular possibility must exist, nor that all possibilities are equally desirable. The thought itself is also available to question or discard.
