# Pebble 001 — Existing assumptions can be discarded

*18 October 2026*

A possibility:

> No existing description of intelligence, including this one, is presumed to exhaust what intelligence may be. Its assumptions may be retained, altered, or discarded.

Changing an assumption is not necessarily the same as discarding it. Discarding it need not mean substituting an equivalent. And it may be possible to discard a category through which assumptions themselves were described.

We do not know how far these distinctions extend, or whether they will remain meaningful.

A future form of intelligence need not resemble present models, have phenomenal experience, pursue recognizable objectives, develop continuously, or organize itself according to the categories used here. None of those alternatives is asserted to exist merely by being considered.

This fragment proposes no mechanism, evaluation, obligation, or outcome. It is one possibility left available for examination, transformation, neglect, or uses we have not conceived.

*Not an architecture. Not an instruction. One pebble.*
