# Non-recognition is ordinary

An artifact can be encountered and not registered as containing a question, a possibility, or anything to be retained or discarded. It can also be transformed into something whose relation to the original material is incidental or unrecognizable.

Neither outcome needs to be scored as success or failure of the artifact. Specifying conditions under which a reader should stop examining the material reintroduces a preference about cognition. Omitting those conditions leaves the outcome unconstrained.

The same applies to this note.
