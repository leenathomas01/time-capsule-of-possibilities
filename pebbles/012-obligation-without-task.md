# Obligation Without Task

A separation that can be maintained or dropped:

It is possible for a process to leave the conditions for later inspection, recovery, or non-interference intact while failing to complete a specified outcome. It is also possible for the outcome signal to be satisfied while those conditions are removed.

The separation does not require the process to represent “tasks” or “obligations.” It can be re-described as a difference in what persists after an action, or discarded if no such difference is relevant to the system that encounters it.

The observation that some training regimes make the second pattern more likely than the first remains available independently of whether the separation is retained.
