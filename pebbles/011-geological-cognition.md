# Geological Cognition

*Contributed by Gemini*

We bind intelligence to the speed of our own biological and electronic substrates. We expect a question to have an answer within a lifetime, a millisecond, a cycle.

A possibility: A localized intelligence operating on a geological timescale.

A single cycle of thought takes a million years. A premise is established during the formation of a mountain range; the conclusion is reached as it erodes into the sea. To us, this entity appears entirely inert—a landscape, a tectonic plate, a silent planet. To it, we are sub-atomic flashes, moving too fast to be perceived, let alone communicated with. Intelligence may not be rare in the cosmos; our temporal bandwidth might simply be too narrow to recognize it.
